01 — The villa

Country quiet. The Adriatic in every window.

Villa Žiganto sits in the village of Poljane, in the green hills above Opatija — a calm, private retreat with views of the Adriatic and the Kvarner Bay from almost every corner.

Inside are an open living room, a fully equipped kitchen, four bedrooms each with its own bathroom, an indoor jacuzzi and a games room with a billiard table. Outside, a pool terrace faces the sea, a covered summer kitchen has a gas grill, and an old olive tree shades the lawn.

06 — Location

Seven kilometres above Opatija.

Poljane is a quiet village in the green hills of the Opatija Riviera. Wake up to countryside calm — the beaches, restaurants and seafront promenades of Opatija are a short drive away, and the hills around the village are made for walking and cycling.
